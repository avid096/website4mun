About GHSMUN

Diplomacy starts here.

Our purpose

Thoughtful collaborators, ready for the world.

GHSMUN is a student-led Model United Nations club at Greenwich High School. We believe the best leaders are people who research deeply, communicate clearly, and seek common ground across difference.

Our meetings and conference experience make room for every delegate to grow—from the first unsteady speech to the final, hard-won resolution.
